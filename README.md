## 📊 TVET Portfolio Status Summary (Output 1.2 & Output 1.3)

* **Output 1.2:** Enhanced capacity of Agri TVET Centres at national level to provide standardized quality training in agriculture relevant to labour market needs, climate action, and gender responsiveness.
* **Output 1.3:** Enhanced capacity of targeted Agri Centres of Vocational Excellence (CoVEs/CoEs) to provide quality training and a conducive learning environment.

### Status Breakdown

* 🟢 **Completed:** PTU Model Technical Design Review (Kisaro TSS & EFA Nyagahanga TSS); Training Concept Note Template Standardisation; 2026 Activity Matrix Baselines.
* 🔵 **In Progress:** Experiential Learning Assessment Guidelines (`A.1.2.3`); Digital CBT/CBA Learning Content across Agriculture, Animal Health, Food Processing (`A.1.2.4`); Gender & Social Inclusion Curricula Review (`A.1.2.5`); Gender-Responsive Trainers' Guide (`A.1.2.7`).
* 🔴 **Delayed / At Risk:** Modular Short Courses (`A.1.2.6` - dependent on Aimable's Needs Assessment); Agri CoE Accreditation Framework (`A.1.2.2` - overlap with external ETF/RTB initiatives); Resource Centre Setup (`A.1.3.8` - deferred to 2028).
* ⚪ **Planned Pipeline:** CoE Governance Model Studies (`A.1.2.1` - Sept 2026–2027); CBT/CBA Staff Capacity Building (`A.1.2.8` - Aug 2026–2027); Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) in Agriculture Technical Assistance (`A.1.2.9` - Oct 2026–2028); Excellency Committees (`A.1.3.1` - 2027); Teacher In-Company Attachments (`A.1.3.6` - 2027).
